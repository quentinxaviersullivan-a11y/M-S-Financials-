# AI PLUMBING TAKEOVER — PIPEWHISPER

**Codename:** PIPEWHISPER
**Thesis:** Own the first AI-native layer of the plumbing trade. Predict pipe failures 72 hours before they happen. Partner with — not replace — the blue-collar workforce. Build the data moat no one else has.

**One-line pitch:** *"Shazam for pipes — clip-on acoustic sensors and AI that call the plumber before the pipe bursts."*

---

## Why this wins

- **$124B US plumbing market.** Zero AI-native incumbents. Every competitor is a regional contractor with a truck and a wrench.
- **Failures are expensive, predictable, and insured.** $2.6B/yr in municipal water main losses. $13B/yr in commercial building pipe claims. Insurers will pay to prevent these — they already do for everything else.
- **Hardware moat + data moat + labor moat.** Clip-on sensors, proprietary acoustic model, and a branded network of certified PipeWhisper plumbers. Three moats, one flywheel.
- **Blue collar is the last unsaturated vertical for applied AI.** Every AI founder is building chatbots. We're wiring up the pipes under every building in America.

## The 5-year arc

1. **Year 1 — Wedge.** Get 10 commercial buildings on sensors. Prove 72-hour prediction. Build the acoustic dataset no one else has.
2. **Year 2 — Trust.** First insurance underwriting partnership. First certified-plumber training program. 200 buildings.
3. **Year 3 — Leverage.** Plumber network flywheel. Every install generates leads for network partners; every network job generates sensor data. 2,000 buildings.
4. **Year 4 — Municipal.** Water utilities. Federal infrastructure money. 15,000 sensor nodes across three metros.
5. **Year 5 — Category ownership.** "PipeWhisper certified" becomes the HVAC-equivalent accreditation for plumbers. $68M ARR. Defensible.
