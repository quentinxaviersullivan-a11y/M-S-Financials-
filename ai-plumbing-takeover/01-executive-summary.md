# 01 — Executive Summary

## Company
**PipeWhisper, Inc.** — AI-native predictive maintenance for plumbing infrastructure, delivered through a certified plumber partner network.

## The problem
Pipe failures are the most expensive, most common, and most-ignored failure mode in every building on earth.

- **$13B/yr** in US commercial pipe-failure insurance claims (Zurich/Chubb/Travelers data).
- **$2.6B/yr** in US municipal water main breaks (ASCE Infrastructure Report Card).
- **~250,000** water main breaks annually in the US.
- **30%** of a commercial plumber's revenue is emergency callouts — the highest-margin, most hated job in the trade.
- **Zero** AI-native solutions deployed at scale. The category does not yet have a leader.

The trade itself hasn't changed since the 1970s. Contractors still diagnose failures by ear, by hand, and by showing up after a flood.

## The solution
**PipeWhisper** is a three-layer system:

1. **Hardware — the Node.** A $40 BOM / $499 retail clip-on acoustic sensor. Snap it onto any pipe junction. No modification, no shutdown, no plumber required for install. 5-year battery life. IP68. LoRa mesh.
2. **Software — the Model.** A proprietary acoustic ML model trained on failure signatures across copper, PEX, PVC, cast iron, galvanized steel, and more. Detects corrosion, pressure anomalies, leaks, and root intrusions **72 hours before catastrophic failure** with 94%+ precision in pilot data.
3. **Labor — the Network.** A branded, certified plumber partner program. Every alert routes to a local PipeWhisper-certified plumber who handles the fix. We take a 15% referral fee and build the data flywheel. Plumbers get free leads and premium branding.

## Why now

- **MEMS acoustic sensors** dropped below $3/unit in 2023. The hardware is finally cheap enough.
- **Edge AI inference** on low-power MCUs (STM32U5, nRF5340) now runs transformer-based anomaly detection in <200mW.
- **Insurance pressure.** Carriers are demanding proactive water risk mitigation. Chubb and Travelers are already piloting passive leak detectors — we're the predictive layer they'll eventually buy.
- **Labor shortage.** The US is short 550,000 plumbers by 2027 (BLS). Any tool that makes existing plumbers 30% more efficient is existentially valuable.
- **Federal infrastructure money.** $55B in the IIJA specifically earmarked for water infrastructure — municipal buyers have budget right now.

## Why no one else has done it
- **Wrong founders.** Every climate-tech sensor play is built by Stanford PhDs who've never met a plumber. Every plumbing business is built by tradespeople who can't spell "CNN". The winner sits in between.
- **Wrong wedge.** Previous attempts (Flo by Moen, Phyn, StreamLabs) targeted homeowners via retail. We target commercial buildings and insurance partnerships, where the ROI is instant and the sales cycle is rational.
- **No model.** Nobody has built the acoustic dataset. We will own it within 18 months and it compounds from there.
