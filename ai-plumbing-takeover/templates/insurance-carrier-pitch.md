## The loss line item

Water damage accounts for an estimated **$13B in annual US commercial property claims**, per industry aggregate data. Within most carrier books:

- Water damage is the **most frequent** commercial property claim category
- Water damage severity has **increased 17% over the past 7 years** due to building aging, densification, and climate volatility
- Burst pipes, slow leaks, and sewer backups account for **~70%** of the water damage claim count

Internal data at `[CARRIER]` likely confirms a similar distribution. The loss line is known. The loss line has been known for decades. What has not existed until now is a predictive tool with the acoustic precision and operational simplicity to deploy at scale across insured commercial properties.

## What we have built

PipeWhisper is a three-layer system:

1. **Acoustic sensor hardware.** Clip-on, non-invasive, 5-year battery life, IP68, under 90 seconds to install. Fits any pipe from 0.5" to 6". No plumbing modification. No shutdown. No labor cost. A building engineer can deploy 40 sensors in 45 minutes.

2. **Predictive AI model.** Trained on a proprietary dataset of failure acoustic signatures across copper, PEX, PVC, cast iron, and galvanized steel. Detects 12 distinct failure classes — corrosion progression, pressure anomalies, water hammer, micro-leaks, frozen-thaw stress, root intrusions, and more. Current pilot performance: **94% precision at a 72-hour failure horizon, 89% recall**.

3. **Certified plumber dispatch network.** When the system detects an imminent failure, it automatically routes a work order to a PipeWhisper-certified plumber in the building's area. The fix happens before the flood. Loss event avoided.

## Why this matters to `[CARRIER]`

### 1. Loss frequency reduction
In pilot deployments, we project a **35-55% reduction in water damage claim frequency** for instrumented buildings. Even at the low end, this is a multi-million dollar annual savings for any major commercial book.

### 2. Loss severity reduction
When a failure is detected early, the severity is categorically smaller. The difference between a $4,200 repair of a stressed joint and a $280,000 overnight flood of a three-story tenant space is the same failure caught at two different moments.

### 3. Underwriting differentiation
`[CARRIER]` can offer a **premium credit** (or, internally, a risk-class improvement) to insureds that deploy PipeWhisper in their buildings. This:
- Attracts the best commercial risks (well-run buildings self-select)
- Creates stickiness (instrumented buildings are harder to lose in renewal)
- Generates a proprietary data feed `[CARRIER]` can integrate into its own underwriting models

### 4. Regulatory positioning
As insurance departments in several states begin to require proactive loss-mitigation disclosure, `[CARRIER]` will benefit from having an AI-native prevention program it can point to. First-mover carriers will define the regulatory standard.

## The proposed co-pilot

### Structure
- **Duration:** 12 months
- **Scope:** 5-10 buildings selected jointly from `[CARRIER]`'s current insured commercial property book
- **Selection criteria:** mid-size commercial office, hospitality, or healthcare, 5+ floors, built before 2000 (higher failure rate = faster signal)
- **Sensor count:** ~30-40 sensors per building

### Economics
- **`[CARRIER]` funds:** ~$180K total for hardware, installation, and 12 months of monitoring
- **PipeWhisper provides:** all hardware, installation, monitoring, alerts, dispatch, monthly reports, and an actuarial-grade outcome analysis
- **Sharing:** `[CARRIER]` retains all insurance outcome data; PipeWhisper retains all acoustic telemetry data

### Success metrics
At month 12, we produce a jointly-authored report measuring:
1. Number of alerts issued
2. True positive / false positive breakdown
3. Documented prevented failures (with dollar value estimated using `[CARRIER]`'s historical claim curve)
4. Return on investment for `[CARRIER]` in prevented claim value
5. Acoustic dataset characteristics that might inform underwriting signals
