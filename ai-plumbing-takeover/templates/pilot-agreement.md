## 1. Purpose

PipeWhisper has developed a proprietary predictive maintenance system for plumbing infrastructure, consisting of clip-on acoustic sensors, gateway devices, a cloud-based analytics platform, and a certified plumber dispatch service (collectively, the "System").

Customer wishes to deploy the System at one or more of its facilities on a pilot basis. This Agreement sets forth the terms of that pilot deployment.

## 2. Pilot scope

### 2.1 Covered facilities
PipeWhisper will deploy the System at the following Customer facilities (each a "Pilot Site"):

| # | Facility name | Address | Approx. sensor count |
|---|--------------|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | `[NAME]` | `[ADDRESS]` | `[COUNT]` |

### 2.2 Equipment
PipeWhisper will provide, install, and maintain the following equipment at each Pilot Site (collectively, "Equipment"):

- PipeWhisper Node sensors (clip-on, non-invasive)
- PipeWhisper Gateway devices
- Associated mounting hardware

All Equipment remains the property of PipeWhisper throughout the Pilot Term and must be returned to PipeWhisper upon termination unless Customer elects to convert to a paid subscription under Section 6.

### 2.3 Installation
Installation will be performed by PipeWhisper personnel or an authorized representative. Installation is non-invasive: the Equipment clips onto existing pipes without cutting, welding, or modification. No plumbing modification, water shutoff, or building disruption is required.

### 2.4 Services
During the Pilot Term, PipeWhisper will provide:
1. Continuous acoustic monitoring of instrumented pipes
2. Predictive failure alerts via email, SMS, and dashboard
3. Web-based dashboard access for up to `[5]` Customer personnel
4. Response and routing of alerts to Customer's existing plumbing contractor (or, at Customer's option, to a PipeWhisper Certified Plumber)
5. Weekly summary reports of system activity

## 3. Pilot Term

### 3.1 Duration
The Pilot Term begins on the Effective Date and continues for `[12]` months unless earlier terminated pursuant to Section 7.

### 3.2 Extension
The Parties may extend the Pilot Term by mutual written agreement.

## 4. Fees

### 4.1 Pilot fees
**Option A — Free Pilot:** No fees during the Pilot Term. Customer provides feedback and access to failure outcome data in exchange for the deployment.

**Option B — Discounted Pilot:** Customer pays `$[AMOUNT]` per sensor per month during the Pilot Term, billed monthly in arrears.

**Option C — Outcome-Based Pilot:** Customer pays `$0` during the Pilot Term. If, during or after the Pilot Term, the System demonstrably prevents a pipe failure (as documented by PipeWhisper and agreed by Customer in writing), Customer pays `$[AMOUNT]` as a one-time success fee.

*The selected option for this Agreement: `[A / B / C]`*

### 4.2 Equipment
No charge for Equipment during the Pilot Term. Customer has no obligation to purchase Equipment.

### 4.3 Third-party costs
Any third-party costs — including but not limited to plumber labor for repairs following an alert, electrical work, or network connectivity — are the responsibility of Customer.

## 5. Data & intellectual property

### 5.1 Customer data
Customer retains ownership of all Customer business data, facility information, and personally identifiable information provided to PipeWhisper ("Customer Data"). PipeWhisper may use Customer Data solely to operate the System and provide the Services.

### 5.2 Acoustic and telemetry data
All acoustic recordings, sensor telemetry, failure signatures, and derived diagnostic data collected by the Equipment during the Pilot Term ("Telemetry Data") shall be owned exclusively by PipeWhisper. Customer hereby grants PipeWhisper a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide license to use, modify, reproduce, and create derivative works from Telemetry Data for any purpose, including training machine learning models and publishing anonymized aggregate findings.

*This is the key clause. It is what makes the pilot valuable to PipeWhisper. Do not weaken it.*

### 5.3 PipeWhisper IP
PipeWhisper retains all right, title, and interest in the System, including all hardware designs, firmware, software, machine learning models, algorithms, and documentation. No intellectual property rights are transferred to Customer under this Agreement.

### 5.4 Aggregated data
PipeWhisper may publish anonymized, aggregated findings from the Pilot Site(s), provided that no such publication identifies Customer or any specific Pilot Site without Customer's prior written consent.

### 5.5 Case study
PipeWhisper may, with Customer's prior written consent (which shall not be unreasonably withheld), publish a case study referencing Customer by name, including metrics, outcomes, and images from the Pilot Site(s).

## 6. Conversion to paid subscription

At any time during or within 30 days after the Pilot Term, Customer may elect to convert the pilot deployment to a paid PipeWhisper Subscription. Upon conversion:

- Equipment remains installed; no re-installation required
- Customer executes PipeWhisper's then-current Master Subscription Agreement
- Pricing shall be no less favorable than the then-current standard commercial tier
- All Telemetry Data, Customer Data, and historical alerts remain available to Customer

**Right of first refusal (optional clause):** If Customer elects not to convert within 30 days of Pilot Term expiration, PipeWhisper may remove the Equipment at its own cost.

## 7. Termination

### 7.1 For convenience
Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon 30 days' written notice to the other Party.

### 7.2 For cause
Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 15 days after receiving written notice of the breach.

### 7.3 Effect of termination
Upon termination:
- PipeWhisper will remove the Equipment from all Pilot Sites within 30 days at no cost to Customer
- Customer will cease use of all PipeWhisper software and dashboards
- PipeWhisper retains ownership of all Telemetry Data collected before termination
- Sections 5 (Data & IP), 8 (Confidentiality), 9 (Warranty Disclaimer), 10 (Limitation of Liability), and 12 (General) survive termination

## 8. Confidentiality

Each Party may disclose to the other confidential business, technical, and operational information ("Confidential Information"). The receiving Party shall:
1. Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party
2. Use Confidential Information only for purposes of this Agreement
3. Protect Confidential Information with at least the same care it uses for its own confidential information, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care
4. These obligations survive for three (3) years after termination of this Agreement

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was known to the receiving Party before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without reference to Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law, provided the disclosing Party receives prompt notice.

## 9. Warranty disclaimer

**THE SYSTEM AND SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" DURING THE PILOT TERM.** PIPEWHISPER MAKES NO WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING WITHOUT LIMITATION WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, OR NON-INFRINGEMENT. PIPEWHISPER DOES NOT WARRANT THAT THE SYSTEM WILL DETECT ALL PIPE FAILURES OR THAT ALL ALERTS WILL BE ACCURATE.

**THE SYSTEM IS A SUPPLEMENT TO, NOT A REPLACEMENT FOR, NORMAL PLUMBING MAINTENANCE.** Customer agrees that it shall not rely solely on the System for plumbing maintenance decisions and shall continue its regular plumbing inspection and maintenance practices during the Pilot Term.

## 11. Indemnification

PipeWhisper shall indemnify and hold harmless Customer from third-party claims that the Equipment or System infringes any US patent, copyright, or trademark, provided Customer (i) promptly notifies PipeWhisper of the claim, (ii) grants PipeWhisper sole control of the defense, and (iii) reasonably cooperates with PipeWhisper.

Customer shall indemnify and hold harmless PipeWhisper from third-party claims arising out of Customer's misuse of the System or breach of this Agreement.

## 12. General provisions

### 12.1 Governing law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

### 12.2 Dispute resolution
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation between senior representatives. If unresolved after 30 days, disputes shall be submitted to binding arbitration in Wilmington, Delaware under the Commercial Arbitration Rules of the American Arbitration Association.

### 12.3 Entire agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings, whether written or oral.

### 12.4 Amendment
This Agreement may only be amended in a writing signed by both Parties.

### 12.5 Assignment
Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that PipeWhisper may assign this Agreement in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets.

### 12.6 Notices
All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth above, with copies to legal counsel as designated by each Party.

### 12.7 Counterparts
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original.
