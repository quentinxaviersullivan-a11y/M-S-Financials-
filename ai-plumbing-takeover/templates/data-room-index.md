# Data Room Index — Seed / Series A

What investors will ask for during diligence. Pre-stage all of this in a structured data room (DocSend, Notion, Google Drive with permissioning, or a dedicated tool like DealRoom) before you start the fundraise. Missing items slow down closes; having everything ready signals rigor.

---

## Rules for the data room

1. **Version control.** Every document has a "last updated" date. Anything older than 30 days raises flags.
2. **Access control.** Different investors get different levels. Tier 1 (signed term sheet or serious diligence) sees everything. Tier 2 (early meeting) sees public + summary. Never share financial projections with anyone who hasn't at least committed to a follow-up meeting.
3. **Track access.** Use a data room tool that shows who viewed what, for how long. Pages they lingered on = questions they'll ask.
4. **Prepare the "cold" list.** 5-10 things an investor will definitely ask for that you haven't pre-staged. Know what they are. Have them ready within 24 hours.
5. **Keep the ugly stuff.** Bugs list, churn history, lost deals memo. Omitting them is worse than having them — seasoned investors will notice.

## The "we have nothing to hide" rule

If a diligence question surprises you in a hostile way, the problem isn't the question — it's your preparation. Every ugly truth should be pre-framed in your data room with context and mitigation. Investors reward candor and punish surprise.

The data room is not a defensive document. It's a narrative told through evidence. Design it that way.
