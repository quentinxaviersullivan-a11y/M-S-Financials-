## 1. Program overview

PipeWhisper operates a predictive maintenance platform for commercial plumbing infrastructure. Through the Platform, PipeWhisper generates diagnostic alerts for pipe failures and routes service opportunities to a network of vetted, trained independent plumbing contractors ("Certified Partners").

This Agreement sets forth the terms under which the Plumber will participate in the PipeWhisper Certified Plumber Program (the "Program").

## 2. Nature of the relationship

### 2.1 Independent contractor
Plumber is an independent business. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, agency, franchise, joint venture, or partnership relationship between Plumber and PipeWhisper. Plumber retains full control over its own business operations, pricing, scheduling (except as provided in Section 5.3), employee hiring, and day-to-day work.

### 2.2 No exclusivity
Plumber remains free to accept work from any source, including PipeWhisper's competitors. PipeWhisper is similarly free to work with any other contractor.

### 2.3 Plumber is not a PipeWhisper representative
Plumber shall not represent to any third party that it is an employee, agent, or official representative of PipeWhisper beyond the scope of the "PipeWhisper Certified" designation granted under this Agreement.

## 3. Certification

### 3.1 Certification requirements
To become and remain a Certified Partner, Plumber must:
1. Hold a valid, current plumbing license in all jurisdictions where it provides services through the Program
2. Maintain general liability insurance of at least `$1,000,000` per occurrence and `$2,000,000` aggregate
3. Maintain worker's compensation insurance as required by law
4. Maintain a minimum rating of `4.0/5.0` on PipeWhisper's internal Customer rating system (applies after first 10 jobs)
5. Successfully complete the PipeWhisper Certified Plumber training program (see Section 4)
6. Pass an annual recertification exam
7. Comply with the PipeWhisper Code of Conduct (Exhibit A)

### 3.2 Certification badge
Upon successful certification, PipeWhisper grants Plumber a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to use the "PipeWhisper Certified Plumber" designation, logo, and badge (collectively, the "Marks") in connection with Plumber's marketing, truck wraps, uniforms, invoices, and website, subject to PipeWhisper's brand guidelines.

### 3.3 Revocation
PipeWhisper may revoke certification — and terminate this Agreement — immediately upon written notice if Plumber:
1. Fails to maintain licensing or insurance
2. Receives three (3) or more substantiated customer complaints in any 90-day period
3. Fails to use the PipeWhisper diagnostic app on a Platform-dispatched job
4. Provides false or misleading information
5. Commits fraud, gross negligence, or willful misconduct
6. Otherwise materially breaches this Agreement

## 4. Training

### 4.1 Initial training
Plumber will complete a two-day PipeWhisper Certified Plumber training program covering:
- How the PipeWhisper sensor system works
- Interpreting PipeWhisper alerts and diagnostic reports
- Using the PipeWhisper mobile app for job dispatch and reporting
- PipeWhisper brand standards and customer experience expectations
- Documenting service outcomes for model training

### 4.2 Training fee
Initial training is provided **free of charge** to Plumber during the first three (3) years of the Program (the "Foundational Period"). After the Foundational Period, PipeWhisper may charge a training fee of up to `$[500]` per certification.

### 4.3 Recertification
Plumber will complete an online recertification module annually, covering platform updates and any changes to standards. Recertification takes approximately 2 hours and is free for Certified Partners in good standing.

## 5. Job dispatch & performance

### 5.1 Alerts and routing
When the PipeWhisper Platform generates an alert at a customer site, the system routes the job opportunity to a Certified Partner in the customer's geographic area based on factors including availability, specialty, capacity, proximity, and rating. Plumber acknowledges that:
1. Job routing is fully automated and PipeWhisper makes no guarantee of any specific job volume
2. Plumber has no obligation to accept any specific job offered through the Platform
3. Job offers are non-exclusive — Plumber may decline without penalty, though repeated declinations may impact routing priority

### 5.2 Acceptance
Plumber may accept or decline a dispatched job via the PipeWhisper mobile app within the response window specified in the job offer (typically 15 minutes for emergency, 2 hours for scheduled).

### 5.3 Performance standards
When Plumber accepts a Platform-dispatched job, Plumber agrees to:
1. Arrive within the time window committed at acceptance
2. Use the PipeWhisper mobile app to check in and check out of the job site
3. Record job outcome, parts used, and failure diagnosis in the PipeWhisper app before leaving
4. Provide the customer with an itemized invoice
5. Upload a photo of completed work
6. Respond to customer rating prompts

### 5.4 Pricing
Plumber sets its own pricing for work performed on Platform-dispatched jobs. PipeWhisper does not dictate Plumber's rates. However, Plumber's rates shall be commercially reasonable and transparent to the customer before work begins.

## 6. Fees and payments

### 6.1 Referral fee
For each Platform-dispatched job successfully completed and invoiced, Plumber shall pay PipeWhisper a **referral fee of fifteen percent (15%)** of the total invoice amount (excluding sales tax and Customer-provided materials, if any).

### 6.2 Payment flow
**Option A — Direct pay:** Customer pays Plumber directly. Plumber remits the 15% referral fee to PipeWhisper within 14 days of receiving payment. PipeWhisper invoices referral fees monthly.

**Option B — Platform pay:** Customer pays PipeWhisper via the Platform. PipeWhisper remits 85% to Plumber within 7 days of customer payment.

*Selected option for this Agreement: `[A / B]`*

### 6.3 Taxes
Plumber is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local income taxes, self-employment taxes, sales taxes, and other taxes arising from its business. PipeWhisper does not withhold taxes from any payments to Plumber. PipeWhisper will issue IRS Form 1099 to Plumber annually if applicable.

### 6.4 No fees outside the Platform
Plumber may continue to receive and work jobs from any non-Platform source without any obligation to pay PipeWhisper a referral fee on such work. The referral fee applies ONLY to jobs routed through the PipeWhisper Platform.

### 6.5 Disputes
Fee disputes must be raised in writing within 30 days of the invoice or payment event. Disputes submitted after that window are waived.

## 7. Customer experience standards

Plumber agrees to uphold the following standards on all Platform-dispatched jobs:
1. **Arrive on time.** Communicate proactively if delayed.
2. **Identify as PipeWhisper Certified.** Wear branded identification or reference the program.
3. **Explain the diagnostic.** Walk the customer through the PipeWhisper alert and the plumbing issue in plain language.
4. **Document outcomes.** Accurate, complete outcome reporting in the app is mandatory.
5. **Respect the property.** Clean up after the job.
6. **No unauthorized upselling.** Plumber shall not use Platform-dispatched jobs as an opportunity for aggressive or deceptive upselling.

Violation of these standards may result in warning, suspension, or termination of the Certification.

## 8. Data

### 8.1 Job outcome data
Plumber acknowledges that its reporting of job outcomes — including diagnosis, repair type, parts used, time spent, and photographs of the pipe condition — is a critical input to PipeWhisper's machine learning model. Plumber agrees to provide this data accurately and promptly for every Platform-dispatched job.

### 8.2 Data license
Plumber grants PipeWhisper a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, store, analyze, and create derivative works from all job outcome data submitted through the Platform for any purpose, including improving the Platform and training machine learning models.

### 8.3 Customer data
Customer names, addresses, and contact information obtained through the Platform are confidential to PipeWhisper and may not be used by Plumber to solicit business outside the Platform for a period of twelve (12) months after the last Platform-dispatched job with that customer.

## 9. Insurance and liability

### 9.1 Plumber insurance
Plumber shall maintain the insurance described in Section 3.1 throughout the term of this Agreement and shall provide a Certificate of Insurance naming PipeWhisper as an additional insured upon request.

### 9.2 Workmanship
Plumber is solely responsible for the quality and safety of its workmanship on Platform-dispatched jobs. PipeWhisper is not responsible for, and disclaims all liability arising from, Plumber's acts, omissions, or workmanship.

### 9.3 Indemnification
Plumber shall indemnify and hold harmless PipeWhisper, its officers, directors, and employees from any claims arising out of (i) Plumber's acts or omissions, (ii) Plumber's breach of this Agreement, (iii) bodily injury or property damage caused by Plumber's work, or (iv) any claim by Plumber's employees or subcontractors.

PipeWhisper shall indemnify Plumber from third-party claims that the PipeWhisper Platform (not including Plumber's work performed using the Platform) infringes any US patent, copyright, or trademark.

### 9.4 Limitation of liability
Neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. PipeWhisper's aggregate liability to Plumber under this Agreement shall not exceed the total referral fees paid by Plumber to PipeWhisper in the six (6) months preceding the claim.

## 10. Term and termination

### 10.1 Term
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either Party.

### 10.2 Termination for convenience
Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon 30 days' written notice.

### 10.3 Termination for cause
Either Party may terminate immediately if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within 15 days of written notice. PipeWhisper may terminate immediately and without opportunity to cure in cases of fraud, safety violations, license lapse, or insurance lapse.

### 10.4 Effect of termination
Upon termination:
1. Plumber's certification and license to use the Marks terminates immediately
2. Plumber must remove all PipeWhisper branding from trucks, uniforms, websites, and marketing within 30 days
3. Plumber must complete any in-progress Platform-dispatched jobs in good faith
4. Outstanding referral fees must be paid within 30 days
5. Sections 6, 8, 9, and 12 survive termination

## 11. Restrictive covenants

### 11.1 Non-circumvention
Plumber agrees not to solicit or accept work directly from any customer introduced to Plumber through the Platform for a period of twelve (12) months after the most recent Platform-dispatched job with that customer, unless such work is dispatched through the Platform.

### 11.2 Non-disparagement
Each Party agrees not to make disparaging statements about the other Party in any public forum.

### 11.3 What's NOT restricted
For the avoidance of doubt, this Agreement does NOT restrict:
- Plumber's right to serve customers it has independently acquired
- Plumber's right to work with other software platforms, dispatch services, or lead-generation tools
- Plumber's general business operations in any way

## EXHIBIT A — PIPEWHISPER CODE OF CONDUCT

Every Certified Plumber agrees to:

1. **Respect the customer.** Treat every customer with professionalism regardless of job size or location.
2. **Be honest about the problem.** Use the PipeWhisper diagnostic to explain the issue accurately. Do not invent problems to upsell.
3. **Quote fairly.** Provide transparent, itemized quotes before beginning work beyond the scope of the original dispatch.
4. **Arrive ready.** Bring appropriate tools and parts for the predicted failure class.
5. **Leave it clean.** Clean up work areas before leaving.
6. **Report accurately.** Enter honest job outcomes in the app. The model only improves if the data is honest.
7. **Wear the badge proudly.** Represent the PipeWhisper brand with professionalism on and off the job.
8. **Refer other Certified Plumbers.** When a job is outside your specialty or geography, route it to another Certified Plumber through the app.
9. **Stay certified.** Maintain licensing, insurance, and training requirements.
10. **Speak up.** Report any safety or ethical concerns to PipeWhisper immediately.

Violation of this Code may result in warning, suspension, or permanent termination of Certification.
