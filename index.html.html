Our Story

Why We Started M&S

M&S Financial Consulting was founded to close a gap that we saw repeatedly throughout our careers: small and mid-sized businesses were making major financial decisions — hiring, investing, acquiring, scaling — without the data, models, or expertise they needed to do it well.

Meanwhile, enterprise companies had entire departments dedicated to financial planning, analysis, and strategy. The disparity wasn't fair, and it wasn't necessary.

We built M&S to bridge that gap — bringing the same rigorous financial thinking that drives Fortune 500 decisions to the businesses that need it most: ambitious entrepreneurs, growth-stage companies, and forward-thinking leaders who are ready to level up their financial operations.

Our Mission: To empower every business with the financial clarity, tools, and strategic guidance they need to make confident decisions, grow sustainably, and achieve lasting success.

⚙ Business Process Solutions

Turn Financial Complexity
Into Operational Clarity

In a volatile market, operational efficiency isn't optional — it's how you survive and thrive. We redesign your core financial processes so your team spends less time fighting fires and more time driving results.

The Challenge

Broken Processes Are Silently Costing You

For most small and mid-sized businesses, financial operations grow organically — a spreadsheet here, a manual process there, a workaround that becomes permanent. Over time, these patches create a system that's fragile, inaccurate, and impossible to scale.

The result: delayed closes, reporting errors, compliance risk, frustrated employees, and leadership teams making decisions without confidence in the numbers.

We identify those gaps, rebuild the workflows, and implement frameworks that are clean, documented, and built to scale with your business.

Signs You Need Business Process Help

  • Month-end close takes more than 10 business days
  • Your team relies heavily on manual spreadsheet processes
  • You've had payroll errors in the last 12 months
  • Leadership doesn't trust the numbers in management reports
  • You're preparing for an audit, investment, or sale
  • Finance headcount is growing but productivity isn't

🤝 Customer Solutions

Your Customers Are Your
Biggest Financial Asset

Profitability doesn't only come from cutting costs — it comes from understanding which customers, channels, and relationships generate the most value. We connect your customer data to your financial strategy.

The Opportunity

Most Businesses Don't Know Which Customers Actually Make Them Money

It's one of the most common and costly blind spots in business: you're spending heavily to acquire and serve customers, but you don't know which ones are profitable, which ones are at risk, and which ones are actually costing you more than they're worth.

We build the analytical infrastructure to answer those questions — and then connect the answers directly to your financial and operational strategy.

Key Metrics We Help You Master

  • Customer Lifetime Value (CLV / LTV)
  • Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) efficiency
  • Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
  • Churn rate & early warning indicators
  • Segment & cohort profitability
  • Customer service cost per interaction

Customer Data & Analytics

  • Customer segmentation frameworks

//Procurement
🔗 Procurement

Transform Procurement From
a Cost Center Into a Strategic Asset

In a high-cost, supply-constrained environment, procurement has never mattered more. We help organizations move beyond transactional purchasing and build intelligent procurement functions that reduce risk and control costs.

The Opportunity

Most Businesses Are Leaving Money on the Table

For many small and mid-sized businesses, procurement is reactive — buying what's needed, when it's needed, from whoever is most convenient. The result is fragmented spend, poor vendor relationships, and a mountain of value being lost every year to inefficiency and missed leverage.

We turn that around. Our procurement engagements are grounded in financial analysis and practical process design — delivering measurable, sustainable cost savings and supply chain resilience.

Common Value Leakage Points We Fix

  • Maverick spend outside approved channels
  • Auto-renewed contracts at unfavorable terms
  • Duplicate vendors performing similar services
  • No visibility into total spend by category or supplier
  • Missing or inconsistent payment terms
  • Poor supplier performance with no accountability
🔄

Procurement Process Redesign

We assess your end-to-end procurement lifecycle and redesign it for clarity, compliance, and efficiency — building a framework that scales as your business grows.

  • Procure-to-pay lifecycle assessment
  • Policy & approval workflow design
  • Supplier onboarding process redesign
  • Procurement governance framework
📋

Contract Lifecycle Optimization

Poorly managed contracts are one of the most common sources of value leakage in organizations. We implement frameworks that improve visibility, enforce compliance, and capture every dollar of contracted value.

  • Contract inventory audit & risk assessment
  • CLM process design & implementation support
  • Renewal calendar & alert system design
  • Contract compliance monitoring
🤖

Technology Enablement & Automation

From e-procurement platforms to AP automation, we evaluate, select, and support the implementation of technology that eliminates manual work and gives procurement real-time visibility.

  • Procurement technology assessment
  • Platform evaluation & selection support
  • AP automation opportunity analysis
  • Implementation project management
📉

Spend Analytics & Cost Reduction

You can't manage what you can't see. We build spend analytics capabilities that reveal exactly where your money is going — then develop targeted strategies to reduce cost without sacrificing quality or supply security.

  • Spend cube build by category, supplier & BU
  • Savings opportunity identification & sizing
  • Supplier rationalization analysis
  • Category strategy development
